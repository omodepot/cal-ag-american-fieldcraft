01 / PERFECT VAN WAMEL

Made in Holland since 1947. Distributed by CAL-AG since 1982.

PERFECT machines are designed and built by Van Wamel B.V. in Beneden-Leeuwen, the Netherlands. In 1982 CAL-AG was appointed importer and distributor of PERFECT brush shredders and rotary mowers for the southwestern United States.

We keep shredder and mower models in stock, carry a complete inventory of replacement parts, and support and service every machine we sell.
