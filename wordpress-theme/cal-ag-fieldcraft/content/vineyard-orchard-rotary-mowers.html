PERFECT VAN WAMEL / VINEYARD & ORCHARD ROTARY MOWERS

Six series, one standard of cut.

PERFECT rotary mowers are built for the grass alleys of orchards and vineyards: generously overlapping, reversible blades, mostly V-belt driven to protect the gearbox from shock loads, with support wheels and rollers that follow the ground.

Pick by the job: variable width (DR) for changing row spacing, skids (LF) for speed, gear drive (LK) for infrequent cutting, double swing arms (MD), twin gearboxes with side discharge (RN), or the extra-low T series under low branches.

SERIES DR

DR variable-width mowers with wings

A rigid central section with a hydraulically adjustable wing on each side, for exact cutting width in orchards and vineyards with different row spacings.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • Central section with a hydraulically adjustable wing on each side
  • Indirect, flexible drive with 2 gearboxes and V-belts
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • 2 front support wheels and 1 rear roller

SPECIFICATIONS

SERIES LF

LF rotary mowers on skids

Long rounded skids allow working speeds around 6 mph; simple and sturdy for grass-strip maintenance in orchards and vineyards, estates, and parks.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • Terrain adjustment and cutting height set by 2 skids
  • Offset possibility

SPECIFICATIONS

SERIES LK

LK gear-driven rotary mowers

Blades driven directly from the gearboxes, with rubber shock absorbers and a protected driveline, for parcels mowed less often.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Cutting blades driven directly from the gearboxes
  • 3-point linkage, cat. II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • PTO shaft with friction and overrunning clutch
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • Terrain adjustment and cutting height set by 2 skids
  • Offset possibility

SPECIFICATIONS

SERIES MD

MD rotary mowers with two swing arms

A central section flanked by two 24″ swing arms for complete weed control from the center row to the tree row in one pass.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • 2 swing arms, 24″
  • Indirect, flexible drive through 2 gearboxes and V-belts
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • 2 front support wheels and 1 rear roller

SPECIFICATIONS

SERIES RN

RN twin-gearbox rotary mowers

Two gearboxes turning in opposite directions improve the cut in taller grass; clippings go behind the mower or onto the tree strips at left and right.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Low, streamlined profile with rounded sides
  • 2 front support wheels and 1 rear roller
  • Discharge behind, or to the left and right
  • Optional manual or hydraulic offset

SPECIFICATIONS

SERIES T

T extra-low rotary mowers

A low, streamlined profile with rounded sides that protects low-hanging branches and fruit.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Low, streamlined profile with rounded sides
  • 1 or 2 front support wheels and 1 or 2 rear rollers
  • V-belt drive
