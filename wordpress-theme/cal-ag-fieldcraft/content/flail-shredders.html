PERFECT VAN WAMEL / FLAIL SHREDDERS

Prunings up to 4″, pulverized where they fall.

PERFECT flail shredders chop orchard and vineyard prunings in a single pass, leaving fine material on the orchard floor so nothing has to be raked, hauled, or burned. Heavy 5 lb flails on a balanced rotor do the work; lighter 1¾ lb flails turn the same machine into a grass mower.

Choose by the size of your prunings and how you run your tractors: the low BK2 for most orchards and vineyards, the heavy-duty KK for bigger wood and crop residue, and the BKM when one machine has to work front- or rear-mounted.

SERIES BK2

BK2 low & compact flail shredders

A low, compact frame (only 20″ high) that passes under branches, with a parallelogram offset to reach the tree row and adjustable rear rake tines to lift prunings lying flat.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • 3 V-belts with automatic spring-loaded tensioner
  • Maximum height 20″
  • Mechanical parallelogram-type offset
  • 5 lb flails or 1¾ lb grass flails
  • Rear rake tines

SPECIFICATIONS

*Minimum tractor hp at 540 rpm: grass cutting / brush chopping. Actual requirement depends on the work.

SERIES KK

KK heavy-duty flail shredders

A larger reinforced rotor and gearbox for heavier wood and vegetation residues such as corn stalks. The KK-245HD adds a heavier frame, heavy-duty gearbox, and 8″ rear roller and rotor.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • 4 V-belts with automatic spring-loaded tensioner
  • Maximum height 28″
  • Mechanical parallelogram-type offset
  • 5 lb flails or 1¾ lb grass flails
  • Adjustable rear opening cover
  • Rear rake tines
  • Extra wear plate factory-installed on all models

SPECIFICATIONS

*Minimum tractor hp at 540 rpm: grass cutting / brush chopping.

SERIES BKM

BKM front & rear-mounted flail shredders

One universal headstock fits the front or rear linkage without modification, so the same shredder can move between tractors. A universal gearbox accepts either PTO rotation.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Universal headstock for front and rear linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540, 750 or 1000 rpm
  • Universal gearbox for either PTO rotation, with integrated free-run
  • 3 V-belts with automatic spring-loaded tensioner
  • Maximum height 20″
  • Hydraulic offset, 19″
  • 5 lb flails or 1¾ lb grass flails
  • Rear rake tines

SPECIFICATIONS
