PERFECT VAN WAMEL / SOIL CULTIVATION

Healthy grass strips start below the surface.

Heavy orchard machinery compacts the soil under the grass strip, leading to wet spots, shallow roots, and a strip that can’t carry traffic. PERFECT’s grass-strip tools open and aerate the soil while keeping the sod intact.

For the tree row itself, the Terra Ranger cultivates under the trees at speed, a cost-effective alternative to soil herbicides.

GRASS-STRIP MAINTENANCE

Subsoil, aerate, overseed.

WBG 2

Grass-strip subsoiler

Spring-loaded cutting discs open the sod and tines break compaction down to about 17½″ without mixing the soil; heavy rollers re-firm the strip.

Tine spacing 2′11″ · from 90 hp

GB

Aerator

Four double-edged blades per rotor open the topsoil to about 7¼″ for better air and water infiltration, without damaging the grass.

Working width 4′3″–7′7″ · from 40 hp

GRASS PROFI

Overseeder

Levels, harrows out weeds and shallow grass, seeds, and rolls in one pass.

Working width 5′11″

SOIL CULTIVATION

Disc harrow & in-row tillage.

SKYROS VARIO

Disc harrow

Two rows of serrated discs across the full width, with stepless angle adjustment from cutting to mixing; no disc cleaners needed.

Working depth 4″–6″

TERRA RANGER

In-row cultivator

A reverse-rotating tiller and finger weeder work the tree row at up to 9 mph, with a working angle that follows uneven orchards.

Working width 8′1″–11′4″ · from 50 hp
