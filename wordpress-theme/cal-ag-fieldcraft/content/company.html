THE CAL-AG STANDARD

Born close to the work. Built in the San Joaquin Valley.

CAL-AG Enterprises, Inc. was founded in 1969 by Robert “Bob” Sparks in the rural farming community of Dinuba, California. The company has always focused on the specialized implements used in orchard and vineyard maintenance.

Today we work from Fowler, about ten miles south of Fresno, as a small team that works hand in hand with our manufacturers to deliver complete satisfaction with both products and service.

HISTORY

Five decades in the field.

  • 1969 Robert “Bob” Sparks establishes CAL-AG in Dinuba, California.
  • 1982 CAL-AG becomes the importer and distributor of PERFECT Van Wamel brush shredders and rotary mowers, made in Holland, for the southwestern United States.
  • 1994 After Bob Sparks passes away in January, his wife Cecilia “Cis” Sparks takes over, with Jose M. Sanchez as General Manager.
  • 2005 CAL-AG moves to its current home in Fowler, about ten miles south of Fresno.
