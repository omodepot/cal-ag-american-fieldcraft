THE CAL-AG STANDARD

Born close to the work. Built in the San Joaquin Valley.

CAL-AG Enterprises, Inc. was founded in 1969 by Robert “Bob” Sparks in the rural farming community of Dinuba, California. The company has always focused on the specialized implements used in orchard and vineyard maintenance.

Today we work from Fowler, about ten miles south of Fresno, as a small team that works hand in hand with our manufacturers to deliver complete satisfaction with both products and service.

HISTORY

Five decades in the field.

  • 1969 Robert “Bob” Sparks establishes CAL-AG in Dinuba, California.
  • 1982 CAL-AG becomes the importer and distributor of PERFECT Van Wamel brush shredders and rotary mowers, made in Holland, for the southwestern United States.
  • 1994 After Bob Sparks passes away in January, his wife Cecilia “Cis” Sparks takes over, with Jose M. Sanchez as General Manager.
  • 2005 CAL-AG moves to its current home in Fowler, about ten miles south of Fresno.

OUR TEAM

The people behind the machines.

PRESIDENT

Cecilia “Cis” Sparks

Cis became involved with CAL-AG when Bob passed away in January 1994, with very little knowledge of the business itself. Bob’s warehouse person, Jose Sanchez, then only 25, was invaluable in helping her learn it, and against all odds they kept the company running successfully.

Today she is the “internal affairs go-to person”: office manager, banking, and accounts receivable and payable. She is President and majority shareholder of CAL-AG Enterprises, Inc.

SECRETARY & GENERAL MANAGER

Jose M. Sanchez

Jose joined CAL-AG in 1989, stocking parts, preparing parts orders for customers, assembling shredders, mowers, and sprayers, and maintaining the warehouse. In the summer of 1994 he became General Manager, Machine Sales and Service.

His relationships with wholesale and retail customers are built on trust and reliability; as customers put it, if you want a vendor who will be there for you, Jose and CAL-AG are it. He is a minority shareholder and Secretary.

VICE-PRESIDENT & WAREHOUSE MANAGER

Carlos H. Sanchez

Carlos joined in 1997 as Warehouse Parts and Service Manager: assembling machines, controlling inventory, and receiving and filling parts orders. Most important is his ability to ask customers the right questions, understand what they need, and give them an answer that works.

He also performs in-shop and, when necessary, in-field repairs and service for all of CAL-AG’s product lines. He is a minority shareholder and Vice-President.
