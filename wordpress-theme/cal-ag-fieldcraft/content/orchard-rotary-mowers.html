PERFECT VAN WAMEL / ORCHARD ROTARY MOWERS

Around every trunk, without touching the tree.

PERFECT orchard rotary mowers carry a spring-loaded swing arm that mows between and around tree trunks. When its rubber-covered wheel touches a trunk, the arm and its blade deflect away from the tree, then return automatically once past it.

The mounted RF suits most orchards; the trailed ZA adds a wider cut and a movable drawbar for larger tree spacing. RF models are also widely used along fences and under solar panels.

ZA pull-type rotary mowers with one swing arm

A trailed mower with a 24″ swing arm and a large offset, for orchards where trees are planted farther apart.

SERIES RF

RF rotary mowers with one swing arm

A 3-point mower with a parallelogram offset and swing arm, for mowing tree rows, fence lines, and under solar panels.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • Swing arm to mow around tree trunks
  • Mechanical parallelogram-type offset
  • V-belt drive
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • 2 front support wheels and 1 rear roller

SPECIFICATIONS

RF FOR SOLAR & FENCES

Under panels, along fences.

The RF’s large offset and swing arm make it a popular choice for mowing between and under solar panels, under board fences at least 10″ high, along wire and rock fences, and around posts. On solar farms the swing arm deflects away from each panel stand on contact.

SOLAR FARMS

Under the panels

A large parallelogram offset reaches under the array while the tractor stays in the alley.

SOLAR OPTIONS

Built for the stands

Options for RF and MD include hydraulic offset, rubber protection flaps, a mechanical-hydraulic feeler, a raisable swing arm, hydraulic swing-arm width, and full rubber wheels.
