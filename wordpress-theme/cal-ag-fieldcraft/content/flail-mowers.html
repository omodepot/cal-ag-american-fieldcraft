PERFECT VAN WAMEL / FLAIL MOWERS

Compact flail mowing for row middles and grounds.

A flail mower cuts with many small swinging flails instead of large blades, giving a fine, even finish and handling debris safely. The compact PERFECT KD series uses 1¾ lb flails on a balanced rotor for clean mowing of row middles, grass strips, and professional grounds.

For heavier wood and prunings, see the flail shredders; for side mowing of banks and ditches, see the multi mowers.

CHOOSING A MOWER

Flail or rotary?

Choose a flail mower

When you want a fine, mulched finish, mow through debris and prunings, or work near people and structures: flails throw less material than rotary blades.

Choose a rotary mower

When you need wide coverage, low profiles under branches, or swing arms that mow around every trunk. See the orchard and vineyard rotary mowers.
