PERFECT VAN WAMEL / MULTI MOWERS

Side, rear, banks, and ditches.

PERFECT multi mowers are flail mowers on an articulating arm: the cutting head works behind the tractor or beside it, tilting from +90° to −60° to follow banks, ditches, and road edges. A breakaway system protects the head if it hits an obstacle.

The ZW is the heavy-duty choice for professional use; the Greenflex is a simpler basic-line multi mower for grassland and ditch banks.

SERIES ZW

ZW multi mowers

An extra heavy-duty headstock, double-skin cutting head, and hydraulic offset up to 5′5″.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Extra heavy-duty headstock; 3-point linkage, cat. II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • PTO shaft with overrunning clutch
  • 3 or 4 V-belts
  • Hydraulic offset, max. 5′5″
  • Cutting head articulation +90° to −60°
  • Break-bolt protected safety breakaway
  • Tractor hydraulics: 1 x double-acting, 1 x single-acting (½″)
  • Double-skin metal plate on the cutting head body
  • 1¾ lb flails

SPECIFICATIONS

BASIC LINE

Greenflex multi mower.

A simple, powerful multi mower for grassland and ditch banks that shreds plant debris up to about 1¼″. It works alongside or behind the tractor (+90° to −45°) on a cat. I/II linkage at 540 rpm, with a three-belt drive and rubber discharge protection.
