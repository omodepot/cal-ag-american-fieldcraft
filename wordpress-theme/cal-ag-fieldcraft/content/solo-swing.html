PERFECT VAN WAMEL / SOLO-SWING

A swinging disc for trees, posts, and panels.

The PERFECT Solo-Swing is a hydraulically driven swing arm that mows around trees, fence posts, and solar panel supports automatically. Swing-away blades add protection in rough or stony ground, and a support saucer with a ball bearing carries the blade.

Mount the SZA on an existing PERFECT rotary or flail mower, or choose the front-mounted SZF / SZL with its own hydraulic offset and tandem wheels.

MODEL SZA

SZA Solo-Swing attachment

An attachment that adds a hydraulic swinging disc to a rotary or flail mower.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Swing-away blades
  • Support saucer with ball bearing under the blade
  • Carriage beam
  • 3 hoses, 98″ (250 cm)
  • Tractor hydraulics: 1 x double-acting + 1 x free-flow return (½″)
  • Oil flow: 8 gal/min maximum

SPECIFICATIONS

MODELS SZF / SZL

SZF / SZL front Solo-Swing

A front-mounted unit with hydraulic parallelogram offset; no front 3-point linkage needed. SZF suits narrow tractors, SZL standard tractors.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • Swing-away blades, 24″ cutting width
  • Tandem wheels
  • Support saucer with ball bearing under the blade
  • Mounting plate for tractor front mount
  • Hydraulic parallelogram-type offset
  • SZF: max. 5′5″ from tractor center
  • SZL: max. 6′11″ from tractor center
  • Tractor hydraulics: 2 x double-acting + 1 x free-flow return (½″)
  • Oil flow: 8 gal/min maximum
