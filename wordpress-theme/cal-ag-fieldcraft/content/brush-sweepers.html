PERFECT VAN WAMEL / BRUSH SWEEPERS

Clean tree rows, ready for the shredder.

PERFECT brush sweepers clear prunings and leaf residue out of the tree and vine rows and into the alley, where the shredder can reach them. Clean strips also discourage fungus and support effective weed control.

Hydraulically driven, five elastic brushes spin at about 150 rpm with adjustable working angle and height. Choose a single sweeper for one row or a double unit to clear two rows in one pass.

MODELS SPA / SPF

SPA & SPF single brush sweepers

The SPA mounts on a PERFECT flail shredder; the SPF mounts on the front of the tractor with its own carriage beam.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 5 brushes
  • Support wheel
  • SPA: mounting plate for a PERFECT flail shredder
  • SPF: carriage beam for tractor front mount
  • 2 hoses with ½″ quick-disconnect couplings
  • Adjustable working angle
  • Oil flow: 8 gal/min maximum
  • Tractor hydraulics: 1 x double- or single-acting + 1 x free-flow return (½″)

SPECIFICATIONS

MODELS SPD / SPDA

SPD & SPDA double brush sweepers

Two sweeper heads clear two rows in one pass. The SPD mounts on the tractor front with hydraulic width adjustment and lift; the SPDA mounts on the rear 3-point.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 2 x 5 brushes
  • 2 support wheels
  • SPD: front carriage beam, 2 hydraulic rams for width and 2 for lifting, 8 hoses
  • SPDA: 3-point headstock, mechanical width adjustment, 2 hoses
  • ½″ quick-disconnect couplings
  • Adjustable working angle
  • Oil flow: 8 gal/min maximum
  • Tractor hydraulics: SPD 4 x double-acting (or 3 x DA + free-flow return); SPDA 1 x DA or SA + free-flow return
