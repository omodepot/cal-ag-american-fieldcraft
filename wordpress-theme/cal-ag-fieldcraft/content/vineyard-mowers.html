PERFECT VAN WAMEL / VINEYARD MOWERS

Center row to vine row, in one pass.

The PERFECT FV2 vineyard mower pairs a central mowing section with two swing arms that reach into the vine rows, so the alley and both under-vine strips are mowed in a single pass. Each swing arm deflects at a light touch of the trunk and swings back automatically.

With eight sizes from 3′5″ to 12′0″ and optional hydraulic width adjustment, the FV2 fits narrow wine-grape rows and wider table-grape and orchard spacings alike.

SERIES FV2

FV2 vineyard mowers with two swing arms

Indirect, flexible drive through two gearboxes and V-belts protects the driveline; overlapping reversible blades leave a clean cut without striping.

STANDARD FEATURES

  • 3-point linkage, cat. I + II
  • PTO speed 540 rpm
  • Central section with 2 swing arms (13″ or 16½″) to mow from the center row to the vine rows in one pass
  • Indirect, flexible drive through 2 gearboxes and V-belts
  • Overlapping reversible blades
  • 2 front wheels and rear roller

OPTIONS

  • Raisable swing arms
  • 2 rams for hydraulic width adjustment of the swing arms, with 2 hoses and ½″ quick-disconnect couplings
